How Talent Token works

The Journey Pass is the free front door. Behind it sits a system that lets a community find, verify and back gifted people, starting with soccer and growing into music, academics, business and ministry.

Scouts nominate

Holders of a Talent Scout Node put a talent forward.

Nodes vote

One vote per node decides who moves on.

Identity and age

An ID check confirms the talent is 18 or older. Minors are never listed.

Talent consents

The talent signs their own consent from their own wallet.

Journey opens

Supporters claim passes and follow verified milestones.

Talent Scout Nodes

Only 5,000 will ever exist, fixed in the code. A node gives one vote on talent nominations and on milestone payouts. It earns no yield, rewards or revenue share, and transfers stay locked at launch.

Opening price is $500 for nodes 1 to 100, rising $100 after every 100 sold. The sale is not open: it stays paused until an outside security audit and legal review are complete.

Built-in protections

No money in the pass

The pass contract has no paid function and refuses any payment sent to it.

No share of earnings

Nothing here pays anyone from a talent's wages, transfer fees, prizes or royalties.

Adults only, by consent

Real profiles need the node vote, an 18+ ID check and the talent's own on-chain consent.

Split duties

The admin proposes milestones; a separate verifier approves or rejects them.

No personal data on-chain

Profiles and proofs are stored as links and fingerprints only.

Test network first

Everything runs on Base Sepolia until an outside audit is done and a multi-signature wallet holds admin.

Questions

Does the pass cost anything?

No. Claiming is free. On the test network the small gas fee is paid in free test currency from a faucet.

Can I sell or give away my pass?

No. Each pass is locked to the wallet that claimed it, so it always shows who supported early.

Who is the athlete in the demo?

A fictional player with test data. Real athletes appear only after they pass the ID and age check and give their own consent.

Where do I get test currency?

From a Base Sepolia faucet such as the one in the Coinbase Developer Platform, then switch your wallet to Base Sepolia.
